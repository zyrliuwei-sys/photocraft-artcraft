## Email the maintainer

For questions about this website, email **[support@photocraftartcraft.com](mailto:support@photocraftartcraft.com)**. This inbox is the direct contact for the independent developer who maintains the site.

We usually reply within 2 business days. If your message needs further investigation, we will let you know rather than promise an immediate resolution.

## Corrections and website questions

Please include the page URL, the passage or link you are asking about, and what you expected to find. For download-link problems, include your operating system and the package name. For a factual correction, a link to the relevant official documentation or GitHub release helps us review it.

Do not send passwords, payment information, or confidential artwork. If an example image would help, start by describing the problem and use a non-sensitive sample you are entitled to share.

## Contacting the software project

This is an independent third-party guide, not official ArtCraft or PhotoCraft support. We are not affiliated with or endorsed by the projects or their development teams. For software bugs and feature requests, use the [official PhotoCraft issue tracker](https://github.com/storytold/photocraft/issues) after checking for an existing report.

Read [About this site](/about) for our editorial approach and the [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy) for how messages and site information are handled.
